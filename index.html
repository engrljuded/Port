From electrical engineering to project execution.

I'm an Electrical Engineer (Western Mindanao State University, 2013–2018) working across three connected disciplines: industrial electrical construction, solar electrical systems, and electrical cost estimation. My foundation is construction — as an Electrical Works Engineer I supervised electrical works, reviewed drawings, processed change orders, and prepared quantity take-offs and cost estimates on an industrial cement plant project.

In 2024 I added hands-on, field-based solar PV training to that foundation — system design, installation practices, and live product demonstration. Solar is a developing part of what I do, not the whole of it: my core identity is electrical engineering, applied to construction execution and project costing, with solar as a specialised, growing capability.
